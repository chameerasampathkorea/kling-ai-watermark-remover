Kling AI watermark remover FAQ

How do I remove the Kling AI watermark?

Open this Kling AI watermark remover, choose your Kling video or image and the Kling AI logo is detected and removed automatically. Tick the permission box and export. Everything runs in your browser.

How do I remove the Kling AI watermark from a video?

Drop the MP4, WebM or MOV file into the tool. The Kling AI watermark is detected automatically, whatever the video size, then press Export. The logo is removed on every frame while the clip plays and the video is saved with its original audio. Clips up to 3 minutes and 200 MB are supported.

Is this Kling AI watermark remover free?

Yes. It is completely free with no sign-up, no daily limit and no watermark of our own added to the result.

Can I remove the watermark from Kling AI images?

Yes. PNG, JPG and WebP images up to 25 megapixels work. JPG files are exported as JPG and other images as lossless PNG.

Where is the watermark on Kling AI videos?

The visible Kling AI logo usually sits in a corner of the frame, most often the bottom-right. The tool finds it automatically; if a clip is unusual, switch the method to Soft fill and move the box yourself.

Do I need to install an app or extension?

No. The tool works in Chrome, Edge, Safari and Firefox on desktop and mobile. There is nothing to install.

Are my Kling AI videos uploaded to a server?

No. Files are processed on your own device with HTML5 Canvas. Nothing is uploaded, stored or shared.

Will removing the watermark reduce video quality?

Only the marked area is changed. You can export at up to 1080p. Fine detail that was hidden under the logo cannot be recovered, so busy backgrounds may show a soft patch.

Does a paid Kling AI plan remove the watermark?

Kling AI offers paid membership plans, and watermark-free downloads are usually part of them. Plans change, so check the current Kling AI pricing page before you buy.

Is it legal to remove the Kling AI watermark?

Only edit videos and images you created or have permission to change, and follow Kling AI’s terms of service. Some countries require AI-generated content to stay labelled, so check the rules where you publish.
